FAQ

Questions, answered

Which cities do you cover?+

Headquartered in Rajkot, we plan and execute events across Gujarat, India and international destinations.

Do you offer end-to-end planning?+

Yes. Concept, decor, catering, hospitality, photography, sound, lighting and on-day coordination — all under one roof.

How early should we book?+

For weddings we recommend booking 4–8 months in advance. Corporate events typically need 4–10 weeks.

Can you customise packages?+

Absolutely. Every event is bespoke. Share your vision and we'll craft a proposal around it.
