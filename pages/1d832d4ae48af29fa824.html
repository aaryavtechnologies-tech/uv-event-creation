Who We Are

A studio built on passion & precision

UV Events & Creations was built with a vision to transform ordinary celebrations into unforgettable experiences. Founded by Ujala Singh Tomar and Vishal Pathar, the company started with a passion for creating elegant, well-managed and memorable events where every detail matters.

We believe every celebration carries emotions, memories and dreams — and those moments deserve flawless execution. Through dedication, creativity and professional planning, UV Events & Creations has earned trust by delivering premium event experiences with perfection, professionalism and personal attention.

Our Mission

To craft luxurious, deeply personal events that celebrate every emotion with elegance, precision and warmth.

Our Vision

To be Gujarat's most trusted luxury event house — known for cinematic execution, creative design and unwavering integrity.
